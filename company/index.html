StrideAI, Inc. is a Delaware C corporation founded in 2025 and based in Wayne, New Jersey.

Mission

Make the foot a source of everyday movement data, with the wearer in charge of it.

Feet carry the body through every day, yet most movement data is gathered at the wrist or in a lab. StrideAI puts sensing where movement meets the ground, and builds the consent and privacy rules in from the start.

Founder

Mohammed Quadri, MD, MBA

Founder and Chief Executive Officer. Dr. Quadri is a physician executive with more than 20 years of healthcare leadership spanning operations, research and innovation, and early-stage company building. He is the named inventor on StrideAI's patent applications.
