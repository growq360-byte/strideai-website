Website privacy notice.

Last updated 2026-11-21.

What this website collects

This website does not set cookies and uses no analytics trackers or advertising tags. Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security. Messages you send are stored in our email mailbox. The website font is served from this website, not from a third party.

Pre-orders and messages

The pre-order and contact forms open a message in your own email app. A pre-order can include your name, the email address you send it from, your US state, product, quantity, who it is for, shoe size (optional), organization (optional), whether you want the monthly roundup, and any note you add; we use it only to manage your pre-order and contact you about it. Pre-orders are not added to the newsletter unless you choose it. Please do not send health or medical information. We receive only what you choose to send, and use it to reply to you. We do not sell personal information.

Newsletter and report list

If you ask to join the monthly newsletter or the Insights report list, we collect your email address and use it only to send you that newsletter or those reports. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or by emailing mquadri@strideai.net. If we use an email service provider, it will act only on our instructions. We do not sell or rent your email address. Every newsletter includes our postal address, StrideAI, Inc., 120 Osage Rd, Wayne, NJ 07470, United States, and a way to unsubscribe. We act on unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.

Booking a call

Booking a call uses Calendly; Calendly's privacy policy applies to what you enter there.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the pre-order or contact information you sent us, or to cancel a pre-order, by emailing mquadri@strideai.net. We respond within 30 days. If you live in a state that gives you more privacy rights, such as the right to access, correct, delete or copy your personal information, or to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising, you can use them by emailing us. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision.

Retention

We delete pre-order details when you cancel, or 24 months after our last contact with you. We keep other messages as long as needed to respond and for our business records, then delete them.

Children

Pre-orders and this website are for adults 18 or older. This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Videos

Podcast videos load from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode only after you press play. Once a video plays, YouTube may store information in your browser under YouTube's own privacy policy.

The app demo

The demo at /app/ runs in your browser on simulated data. It does not send what you enter to StrideAI. The demo keeps your demo choices in your own browser's local storage so the page works; you can clear them by clearing site data in your browser. The StrideAI app will have its own privacy notice and a consumer health data privacy policy, published before the app collects any data. Both will be linked from the home page of this website.

Changes

We may update this notice; the date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact page.
