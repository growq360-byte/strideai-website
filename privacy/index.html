Website privacy notice.

Last updated 2026-11-21.

What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics trackers or advertising tags. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, page requested and time) for security and reliability.

Reservations and messages

The reservation and contact forms open a message in your own email app. A reservation can include your name, organization, country, shoe size, product and quantity; we use it only to manage your reservation and contact you about it. We receive only what you choose to send, and use it to reply to you. We do not sell personal information.

Newsletter and report list

If you ask to join the monthly newsletter or the Insights report list, we collect your email address and use it only to send you that newsletter or those reports. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or by emailing MQ@growq360.com. Emails may be sent through our email service provider, which acts as our processor and handles your address only on our instructions. We do not sell or rent your email address.

Booking a call

Booking a call uses Calendly; Calendly's privacy policy applies to what you enter there.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the reservation or contact information you sent us, or to cancel a reservation, by emailing MQ@growq360.com. We respond within 30 days. Where state privacy laws give you additional rights, we honor them.

Retention

We keep messages as long as needed to respond and for our business records, then delete them.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Videos

Podcast videos load from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode only after you press play. YouTube's own privacy policy then applies.

The app demo

The demo at /app/ runs in your browser on simulated data. It does not send what you enter to StrideAI. The StrideAI app has its own privacy notice, issued with the app, which will address health-related data and applicable state consumer health data laws.

Changes

We may update this notice; the date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact page.
