Executive summary

StrideAI is inviting founding evaluation partners in soccer, basketball, flag football, snowboarding, skiing, skateboarding and BMX freestyle: clubs, boot fitters, brands and adult athletes who want measured fit in the footwear they already use.

Athletes, fitters and footwear brands still judge the most important question in footwear, whether a shoe or boot fits the way this athlete actually moves, mainly by feel. The research says fit is often wrong 1, that women's feet are not scaled-down men's feet 2,3,4, and that the wearable tools to measure inside the shoe are promising but rest on a small evidence base: one 2026 systematic review of wearable foot and ankle function tests found 4 qualifying studies (83 participants) 8.

The StrideAI Smart Insole is designed to fit the shoes and boots athletes already wear and to record plantar pressure, motion, temperature and humidity on both feet on one clock, in the field. Programs can start with one club or fitter and 6 to 12 adult athletes. Section 1 sets out exactly what a founding program delivers and how each result is measured.

1. Founding evaluation programs

Each founding program runs in the athletes' own shoes and boots, in training and fitting sessions, and produces evidence that a club, a fitter and a brand can each check for themselves. Success thresholds are agreed with each partner in writing before the first session, and no results are claimed until the evaluation is complete.

How a program runs

  1. 1. Discovery and written plan Goals, sponsor fit, data terms, costs, responsibilities and timing agreed in a short written plan.
  2. 2. Bench and reference session Cold, impact, moisture and battery checks, then a laboratory agreement session.
  3. 3. Fit and setup Written consent first. Taking part is voluntary. Programs run as research studies are reviewed by an independent ethics board. The insole is fitted in each athlete's own shoe or boot, with their fitter where available.
  4. 4. Controlled runs and normal training Matched runs for repeatability, then normal training with both feet captured. Athletes get their own data and decide what is shared.
  5. 5. Report and decide A program report for each partner. Partners choose the next step: a wider evaluation, a brand-funded program, or nothing further.

3. What StrideAI measures

How we check our measurements

StrideAI measures known markers, and we check each method for agreement with a reference. Our step-timing and movement methods were checked against reference measurements and labels recorded with other instruments in public research datasets. The results come from people held out of each development step.

These checks used public data, not StrideAI hardware. The next step is validation on StrideAI insoles with our own recorded sessions, and results on StrideAI products may differ. Movement results cover walking, running, jumping and kicking; change-of-direction recognition is still experimental. Analytics built on StrideAI's own data, including AI and per-user models, remain on our roadmap. Dataset authors are credited under each license (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0). No dataset is redistributed here; results are StrideAI's own analyses, and the dataset authors do not endorse StrideAI.

"Designed to measure" describes intended capability under evaluation.

Team and field sports

Cutting, jumping and landing all begin at the foot. A tool that works in the cleats and court shoes players already wear lets teams study foot loading within training, technique and footwear choice in real sessions; with the athlete's consent, the same picture can be shared by player, coach and performance staff. Girls' flag football alone grew from 15,716 to 102,360 high school participation entries in four years 5, and every new player needs footwear that fits. Founding programs enroll adult athletes; the growth in younger players shows where fit matters next.

Snowboarding

Riders and fitters tune stance, angles, forward lean, highback and footbed by feel, often over years. StrideAI is designed to put measurement beside that feel: heel-edge and toe-edge loading under each foot, front and back foot balance labeled by stance so data stays correct when a rider goes switch, relative landing pressure time-stamped to sync with video, a pressure map a fitter can read before and after a fit change, and the setup recorded with every session (board, boot, binding, stance, angles, footbed, snow and terrain). The insole reports the underfoot share of edge load; highback and strap loads sit above the foot and are a separate path to explore with riders.

Freestyle and alpine skiing

In a tight shell, the space an insole takes up matters as much as what it measures. Pressure insoles have already been used with motion sensors and GPS to measure skiing forces on the mountain 9, while boot selection still rests largely on expert experience 7. StrideAI aims to make in-boot measurement simple and repeatable for athletes and fitters.

Skateboarding and BMX freestyle

Skaters protect board feel above everything, so a thin profile is a design requirement. BMX freestyle calls for high lower-limb explosive power for jump amplitude 10, and an insole in a flat-pedal shoe can show how load moves through each foot on the pedals and on landing.

Surfing and adaptive sport

Surf is a later research direction, starting with insoles in cold-water booties. StrideAI is not yet rated waterproof; cold and wet operation is a design requirement, with formal environmental testing planned. StrideAI also plans to explore programs with adaptive sport organizations focused on recreation and athletic performance, shaped with the athletes.

5. Built for women's feet from the first capture

Women athletes are intended to be founding voices of this dataset, not a subgroup added later. Women's feet differ from men's in shape, not just size 2,3,4, yet many women ride boots and shoes built on men's or unisex lasts. StrideAI is designed to record sex, sport and footwear for every session from the first capture, so the data that informs the next generation of women's footwear starts with women players, riders, skaters and surfers. The purpose is fit, comfort and performance in the footwear women already choose.
