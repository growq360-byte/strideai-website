Smart footwear and insole market: size, drivers and lessons

StrideAI market brief. Sources checked November 20, 2026.

Market estimates vary widely. Future Market Insights values the smart insole market at USD 210.0 million in 2025, projecting USD 1,128.3 million by 2036 [1]. Fortune Business Insights values smart shoes at USD 549.13 million in 2025, reaching USD 2,011.86 million by 2034 [2]. Grand View Research projected smart shoes at USD 1,103.2 million by 2030 [3]. Scopes, base years and methods differ, so these figures show direction, not a precise size.

Named drivers include remote foot-pressure monitoring, gait tracking in rehabilitation, endurance sports analytics, and the move from lab force plates to wearables [1].

Earlier attempts show the risks. NURVV Limited, maker of NURVV Run insoles, was dissolved in January 2025 [4]. Under Armour connected 1 million shoes to its MapMyRun app, but its plan for app users to drive apparel sales did not pan out [5]. The lesson: data has to drive a decision that a specific buyer values.
