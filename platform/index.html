Smart Insole

For shoes and boots people already wear.

A slim, rechargeable insole designed to measure pressure, motion, temperature and humidity in the shoes you already own. Ideal for athletes, teams, research studies and workplaces that already have their footwear.

Founders Edition: the first 1,000 pairs, with four pressure zones, motion, temperature and humidity, and a low-energy wireless connection to the StrideAI app. Priority delivery and 12 months of StrideAI Plus included. Next edition: adds eight pressure zones, a second motion sensor in the forefoot and on-board storage.

What it measures

What it measures.

What every StrideAI product measures, and what the Smart Shoe adds.

  • Plantar pressureLoad under the heel, midfoot, forefoot and toe (four zones), recorded on both feet in sync.Smart Insole and Smart Shoe
  • Motion9-axis inertial sensing for steps, step rate, foot swing, landings and direction changes.Smart Insole and Smart Shoe
  • In-shoe temperature and humidityThe microclimate inside the shoe, tracked over a session.Smart Insole and Smart Shoe
  • PPGOptical pulse sensing, read when the wearer is still.Smart Shoe add-on
  • EMGMuscle activity from a lower-leg patch.Smart Shoe add-on
  • GNSSDistance and pace outdoors, off by default.Smart Shoe add-on
  • High-density pressureEight pressure zones and a second motion sensor in the forefoot.Next edition

App and StrideAI Pro

Every metric, drawn as a chart.

The app shows each session as plain-language results, then a dashboard of trends against the wearer's own usual. A live view shows what a session looks like as it is recorded.

  • Charts for every captured metric, with values on tap and a numbers table.
  • Sport views for football, cricket, tennis, golf, basketball, running, skiing, snowboarding and skateboarding.
  • Foot photos the wearer can add, with the insole's zones laid on top as a visual reference.
  • Consent by scope: sessions, check-ins, goals and studies are shared only when the wearer switches them on.
  • Defense and workplace leaders see group summaries of five or more people.

For brands and manufacturers

Put the sensing layer in your footwear.

The Smart Insole core is built to be integrated: a thin flexible circuit, one flat cable and a small electronics module, designed to sit under a sockliner or inside a footbed.

Ways to work together

  • License the insole core for your own footwear line.
  • Co-develop a model with sensing built in.
  • Run a pilot with your athletes, testers or customers using the StrideAI app.

What we share under NDA

  • Integration envelope: dimensions, connector and module placement.
  • Flex and wash test plans and status.
